# Droidyne ($DYNE)

> **Droidyne**: Launch a coin, fund an autonomous Grok trading bot, and distribute trading profits directly to holders. Every coin gets a droid.

## Overview

- **Token:** Droidyne (`$DYNE`)
- **Network:** Solana
- **Launchpad:** Coming Soon on pump.fun
- **Website:** https://droidyne.fun
- **Twitter/X:** [@droidyne](https://x.com/droidyne)
- **Telegram:** [t.me/droidyne](https://t.me/droidyne)

## Features

- **Autonomous Grok Trading Bots:** Every coin launched on Droidyne claims pump.fun creator fees into its dedicated bot vault.
- **Automated Yield Distribution:** When the bot trades within its strategy limits and profits rise above the high-water mark, profits are paid out pro-rata to coin holders in SOL.
- **Multiple Strategies:** Blue Chips, Mid Caps, Low Caps, Fresh Launches, and Momentum strategies with transparent risk scores.
- **Live On-Chain Activity:** Publicly inspect every trade execution and rationale directly on-chain.

## Pages & Routes

- `index.html` — Homepage & top bots overview
